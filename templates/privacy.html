Privacy Notice

Effective date: 12 October 2026

This notice explains what information SGILA uses, why it is used, and how a parent, guardian or teacher can ask questions about it. Please read it together with the Terms and Conditions.

Information used by SGILA

Depending on the account and features used, SGILA may process:

  • Parent or teacher name, email address, and optional phone number.
  • Teacher school, grades taught and class information.
  • Learner name, age, grade, school, account details, parent or guardian email address, and any profile photo supplied by an adult.
  • Learner lesson activity, submitted answers, scores, stars and completion progress.
  • Messages, support requests and information submitted through Help and Feedback.
  • Subscription and payment-status information needed to manage a plan. Payment forms may handle payment details; SGILA does not need a user to send full card or bank details through support messages.

The Terms and Conditions state that SGILA does not collect children's voice recordings, location data, device contact lists or biometric information.

Why we use this information

Information is used to create and secure accounts, provide age- and grade-appropriate learning, save progress, show parent and teacher dashboards, link learners to their responsible adults, respond to support requests, and administer subscriptions.

Children and adult responsibility

Children's accounts are created and managed by a parent, legal guardian or teacher. The adult registering a learner should have authority to provide the learner's information and should only enter information needed to use the app. Please do not include sensitive personal information in learner names, answers or support requests.

Cookies and device storage

SGILA uses the browser session and security mechanisms needed for sign-in and form protection. The selected light or dark theme is saved in the browser's local storage. Clearing browser storage may sign you out or reset your theme preference.

Sharing and service providers

SGILA does not sell children's personal information. Information may be handled by hosting, email or other technical providers where needed to run the service, deliver account messages or provide a feature you request. Access should be limited to what is needed for those purposes.

Retention, deactivation and deletion

Account and learning records are retained while needed to provide the service. Deactivating an account pauses access but does not itself erase stored records; some information is retained to support account reactivation and trial eligibility. To request deletion or ask what information is held, email sgila.info@gmail.com from the account email address where possible. We may need to verify the request and the requester’s authority before acting. The Terms and Conditions describe the deletion process.

Your choices and questions

Parents, guardians and teachers can contact SGILA to ask for access to information associated with an account, request correction or deletion, withdraw consent where applicable, or raise a privacy concern. Requests are handled subject to applicable law and verification of the requester's identity and authority. Email sgila.info@gmail.com. Please do not send passwords, full payment card details or a child's sensitive information in an email.

Updates to this notice

This notice may change as SGILA's features or information practices change. The effective date above will be updated when the notice is revised. For questions, contact sgila.info@gmail.com.
