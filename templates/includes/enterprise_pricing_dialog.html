How Enterprise pricing works

SGILA Enterprise is priced per learner, per month. Prices start from R20 per learner per month, with a minimum of 100 learners.

How your price is calculated

  • Per learner, per month: the rate is set according to your school's profile.
  • Total per month: the rate multiplied by the number of learners at your school.
  • Total per year: your monthly total for 12 months.

Example: 100 learners at R40 per learner comes to R4,000 per month, or R48,000 per year.

Who pays: Enterprise is billed to the school, or to a funder acting on the school's behalf, and never to families. It is available to public and independent schools.

Please note: The amounts shown are estimates. Your final price is confirmed when you activate.
