About SGILA

Reading adventures for growing readers

Stories that grow with every reader.

What is SGILA?

SGILA is a digital reading and literacy app for learners in Grades 1–4. It brings together stories and interactive activities to help children practise reading, listening, comprehension, vocabulary, spelling and other literacy skills.

What problem are we solving?

South Africa faces a critical foundational literacy crisis. According to the Progress in International Reading Literacy Study (PIRLS 2021), approximately 81% of Grade 4 learners in South Africa cannot read for meaning — the highest illiteracy rate among all 57 participating countries. This crisis is rooted in the foundational phase (Grades 1 to 4), where children first develop reading comprehension, phonemic awareness and vocabulary.

Who is it for?

Children use the learning activities with support from a parent, guardian or teacher. Adults can create and manage learner profiles, while parents and teachers can follow learning progress through their accounts.

How learning works

  • Learners explore age- and grade-appropriate stories.
  • Interactive activities provide practice and feedback.
  • Progress summaries help families and educators follow completed lessons and scores.
  • Reading and audio activities include English and isiZulu learning content where available.

SGILA is intended as a supplementary learning resource. It supports, but does not replace, teaching, caregiver guidance or a learner’s school curriculum.
