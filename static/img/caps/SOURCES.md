# CAPS story sources

The story text in `seed_data.py` is adapted for SGILA's page size and reading flow from the curriculum-aligned Big Books supplied with the project.

- Grade 1, "A Very Hot Day", "Ben Goes to School", "Olwethu's First Day", and "Bongi Waits": `Grade-1-Term-1-PSRIP-EFAL-Big-Book-2.pdf`
- Grade 2, "A Dog's Life", "The School Shed Is on Fire", and "A New Baby": `Gr02_BB3_PRINT_English.pdf`
- Grade 2, "Dan's Bad Week" and "Spring Day Surprise": adapted from `Grade-1-Term-1-PSRIP-EFAL-Big-Book-2.pdf` with Grade 2 comprehension demands.
- Grade 3, "Mandu's Secret Diary", "Soccer Trouble", "The River Mistake", "Stage Fright", and "The Juice Spill": `Gr03_BB3_PRINT_English.pdf`
- Grade 4, "The Skateboard Shortcut", "The Jersey That Did Not Fit", "Eyes in the Cupboard", and "The Crushed Birthday Flowers": adapted from narrative episodes in `Gr03_BB3_PRINT_English.pdf` and recalibrated to the supplied Grade 4 CAPS FAL comprehension outcomes.

The app contains exactly five stories per grade. Comprehension activities are calibrated against the supplied CAPS English Home Language Grades R-3 document and the Grade 4 section of the supplied CAPS Intermediate Phase English First Additional Language document. Grade R, Grade 5, and Grade 6 content is intentionally excluded.
