# SGILA storyboards

These 18 text-free, six-panel storyboards were generated for SGILA with OpenAI ImageGen on 2026-08-04.

Each prompt specified the story's six narrative beats, consistent South African characters and settings, a 3-by-2 panel layout, a hand-painted digital gouache style, and no words, captions, speech bubbles, labels, logos, or watermarks. The app crops one panel at a time so learners see a distinct illustration beside the separate read-along text.

Files use grade and story slugs, for example `g1_hot_storyboard.png` and `g4_flowers_storyboard.png`.
